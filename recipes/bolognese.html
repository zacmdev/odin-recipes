Spaghetti Bolognese

Description

The GREATEST Spaghetti Bolognese recipe the world has ever seen. Only using a few readily available ingredients and one soul of a freshly killed unicorn, you too can have this special meal all dished up and ready for the next presidential debate!

Ingredients

  • Beef and pork mince at a ration of 69:31
  • Spaghetti handmade from your favourite classmate's Nona down the street
  • Parmesan shaved from the soles of Michael Corleone's first wife (pre-incineration)
  • Tomatoes crushed by the very soles of previous said first wife (post parmesan foot shaving)
  • And of course the soul of a freshly killed unicorn (I mostly get mine store bought but don't tell Tommy's Nona ;3)

Steps

  1. Boil your pot of water but specifically over a flame sourced from Prometheus himself.
  2. While the water is boiling get that mince thrown in a pan and don't touch it. You want that bad boy to set off the smoke alarms.
  3. Once there's not an ounce of colour left in the meat but black, pour in the crushed tomatoes and sprinkle just a pinch of the of the shaved sole parmesan and use your hands to waft in that footy goodness.
  4. Let the meat and tomatoes simmer and throw in your pasta while lifting up your shirt and patting your belly and repeating "Baby wants the boly" about 47 times. Around this time the spaghetti will be perfect and you don't want to drain the water, you want to pour everything directly onto the floor (including the meat and tomatoes).
  5. You then take the rest of the sole parmesan and sprinkle it over the art installation you've just made on your floor and do one final "Baby wants the boly" for good measure.
  6. You then take the unicorn soul out of its bottle (be careful not to fall in love) and you gently place it on top until it disolves into an ethereal goop.
  7. You stare intently at the goop until it shifts from it's milky whiteness into an almost mauve colour. The second it shifts you dive to the floor on all fours and proceed to shove your face into the mess and inhale the meal as quickly as possible (you have about 17 seconds before the unicorn soul posseses your mortal meat suit and starts walking around running erands in your body).
  8. Success! Afterwards, lay down and roll around in the sauce to celebrate a job well done and to stave off any potential neuromuscular tremors that can occur due to the excess foot extract that can occur in the parmesan.
